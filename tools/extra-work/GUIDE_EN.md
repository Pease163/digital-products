# Extra Work Calculator: a clear result in 5 minutes

1. Open `index.html`. Enter a 12,000 RUB budget and 12 base hours. The original rate is 1,000 RUB/hour.
2. Add 6 extra hours. With an unchanged budget, the result is 666.67 RUB/hour: a 33.33% reduction.
3. Leave the new rate blank to use the original rate. Six extra hours cost 6,000 RUB. The agreed-fee scenario is 18,000 RUB / 18 hours = 1,000 RUB/hour.
4. At a custom rate of 800 RUB/hour, the additional fee is 4,800 RUB and the scenario is 16,800 RUB / 18 hours = 933.33 RUB/hour. Proposed fees are not automatically money received.
5. Save TXT or print the result for discussion. This is a scenario, not an invoice, contract or payment receipt.

Currency selection changes the label only. Use actual base hours for an ongoing project, estimated hours for planning, and keep the distinction clear. Negative values and zero base hours are rejected. Taxes, expenses, platform fees and exchange rates are not included. Inputs are neither saved nor transmitted.
